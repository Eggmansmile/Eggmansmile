## Hi, I'm Ruben 👋

I study electromechanical engineering (industrieel ingenieur) at KU Leuven, Group T, in Leuven. My focus is machine and product design. In my free time I volunteer at [Maakleerplek](https://github.com/maakleerplek), a makerspace, where I build the software that runs it.

### What I work on

- **Maakleerplek stock system**: a web app for stock, the till and the laser-cutter service, a Raspberry Pi kiosk (Rust/Slint) and a TV display (Next.js).
- **[Inventree-assistent](https://github.com/Eggmansmile/Inventree-assistent)**: a barcode scanner that removes items from our InvenTree stock.
- **[Samenvattingen KU Leuven](https://github.com/Eggmansmile/Samenvattingen-Ku-leuven-Industriele-ingenieurs-79f60db4)**: course summaries and exercises for industrial engineering, in LaTeX and Typst.
- **[My CV](https://github.com/Eggmansmile/Ruben-Ryckaert-s-CV)**: a React web app, live at [cv.gtvault.dpdns.org](https://cv.gtvault.dpdns.org/).
- **A DIY car**: my own PCB in KiCad and the parts in CAD.
